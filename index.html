My idea

Same engine. Pointed at the supplier file.

I already run a four-step workflow on public filings. It finds a buyer and drafts an email I review before it sends. I came up with the next use myself. Run those same four steps on a listed supplier, and most of the form a brand is about to send is already written.

The workflow I run today

Public filing in. A reviewed email out.

  1. Step 1

    Read the filing

    Public sources only. The workflow starts from documents the company has already published.

    • Annual reports
    • SEC filings
    • Call transcripts
    • ESG disclosures
  2. Step 2

    Pull the signal

    It finds the moment worth acting on, inside a document a person would otherwise skim.

  3. Step 3

    Name the person

    Who owns that problem, and what they have already said on the record.

  4. Step 4

    Draft, then I send

    A first email tied to the filing. I review every draft. Nothing goes out on its own. Reply rates tripled.

Same pages. Same read. I came up with a different handoff: a supplier form, instead of an email.

How I would build it for supplier data

Listed supplier in. A mostly written form out.

  1. Step 1

    Match the supplier

    The brand names the supplier. If that company is listed, or sits under a listed parent, a filing with their name on it already exists.

  2. Step 2

    Read those same pages

    The same pass over the annual report, the SEC filing, and the ESG disclosure. This is the public company. It is a reading of what they already published.

  3. Step 3

    Pre-fill what is public

    Company-level lines land on the brand’s questions, each one tagged with its source. Site meters and SKU-level detail stay blank. A filing does not carry them.

  4. Step 4

    They confirm the rest

    The supplier corrects the public lines and types only the gaps. A person checks the source before it counts. What they have to give the brand drops by a lot.

Today

The form arrives empty. The supplier stalls, estimates, or sends nothing. Scope 3 inherits the guess.

With this idea

The form arrives mostly written, and every proposed line names the filing it came from. The supplier finishes what was never public.
